Start in about a minute

There is no separate account for Open Workbench. It runs inside Claude.

  1. Open the link

    Choose Open Workbench on this page. It opens in a new tab on claude.ai.

  2. Sign in to Claude

    Sign-in happens on claude.ai. Open Workbench never sees your password.

  3. Allow your storage

    Connect Dropbox or Google Drive once in Claude's settings, then allow the page to use them from the Files tab.

What to know before you open it

You need a Claude account
The page runs inside claude.ai, so you sign in there first.
Your work stays with you
Work you do on the public link is saved in your own browser, unless you save files to Dropbox or Drive.
You choose each connection
The page asks before it uses Dropbox, Google Drive or local files, and you can manage them in Claude.
Local files need the desktop app
Open Workbench in the Claude desktop app with the Filesystem extension turned on to use that tab.
